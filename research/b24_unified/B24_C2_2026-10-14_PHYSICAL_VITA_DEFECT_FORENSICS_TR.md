## 6. Ana teknik strateji: tek B24 üzerinde gerçek GPU + animasyonlar
**P0 GPU ownership + input**: `SDL2` controller input ve 480×272 CPU texture present bugün. VitaGL, OpenGL-to-sceGxm wrapper. Yeni B24 tek-sahipli `vglInit/glClear/glDrawElements/vglSwapBuffers` hattına geçmeli (ya da doğrulanmış SDL PVR OpenGL backend), `SDL_Renderer` ile iki farklı context/present'i çakıştırmamalı. Shader/compiler `libshacccg.suprx` gereksinimleri fiziksel Vita'da kontrol edilecek. 16-bit indices doğal yol, büyük meshleri batch et; etkin frustum, inverse bind/anim dynamic vertex buffers. **Önce eski koşan 5 orijinal suit + orijinal C1L1 geometry + HUD'ı TEK GPU pipeline'da göster; sonra texture, input+weapon pose, collision ve gameplay katmanlarını test et.** Harita görünmeden yeni ayrı GPU kare demo VPK kabul edilmeyecek.
**P1 texture**: B12 recovered atlases ve kaynak material node mapping; UV buffers; sky/lightmap/original color matching.
**P1 anim bake**: 304-name census->required clip manifests, high-priority 3 grounded melee, repulsor raised-right hand, chest Unibeam, original WM neutral walk, fly/land, RUSH; all other suit action families. Stop animation restart reentry/air-ground contact early.
**P1 model direction**: B17 world Z-up authoritative; B22 modules Y-up transformed with explicit basis, target and muzzle socket transforms world-space.
**P2 QA**: P0 original B17 feel, no animation/character switch blockers, three-stage flight, pause, GPU perf CPU/GPU/UP/PRES, C1L1 texture scene, 5-suit regression. No arbitrary FPS promise; success device verified.
